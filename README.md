# InstaFinder

Find someone on Instagram by reviewing accounts connected to profiles or posts you know.

Collect **followers, following, commenters and likers**, then review each person’s photo, name and Instagram profile. Accounts with more known connections to your chosen sources appear first.

## How to use it

1. **Create a mission.** Click the extension icon → **Open InstaFinder**, then give your mission a name, such as “Find Alex from Friday’s event”. A mission keeps that goal’s candidates and your decisions together.
2. **Choose a starting point on Instagram.** Open a profile or post that might lead to the person—for example, the event venue’s account. This is your **source**.
3. **Collect candidates.** Click the extension icon and choose **Followers**, **Following**, **Commenters** or **Likers**. Select a mission if you have several. A post or reel offers commenters and likers.
4. **Keep the Instagram tab open.** The collector opens lists, scrolls and expands replies automatically. Use the corner panel to pause, resume or switch to manual collection. You can review candidates while it runs.
5. **Review one person at a time.** Choose **Not the person**, **Probably not** or **Possible match**. Your choice removes them from the review queue; **Undo** brings them back.

Use **Candidates** to search, filter possible matches or change a decision. Add more sources whenever you need to widen the search.

## Install

The Chrome Web Store listing is being prepared. Until it is published, use the [manual installation instructions](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md#install-and-run).

## Your data

Your missions, profiles and decisions stay in your browser. No InstaFinder account, subscription or cloud upload is required. Sign in to Instagram normally in the same Chrome profile.

Export a backup from **Settings & backups** before removing the extension or moving to another installation. Instagram can restrict collection or request a cooldown; saved results remain available.

InstaFinder is an independent tool, not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
